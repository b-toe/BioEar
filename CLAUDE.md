PROJECT: BIOEAR

BioEar is a student engineering project developing a low-cost,
non-surgical bone-conduction hearing-device prototype.

The entire website must be about BioEar.

Never replace BioEar branding with generic healthcare branding.

Design language:
- minimalist
- pastel
- soft
- scientific
- futuristic
- human
- lots of whitespace
- rounded cards
- thin line illustrations
- subtle animations

Primary colors:
- soft lavender
- pale blue
- muted mint
- warm cream
- soft peach
- charcoal text

Avoid:
- hospital blue
- harsh neon
- excessive gradients
- generic medical stock photos
- giant text everywhere
- clutter
- excessive glassmorphism

BioEar should always be the visual and conceptual focus.

The website is an engineering prototype website, not a medical advice site.

Do not make unverified medical claims.

Prefer custom SVG/CSS artwork.

When external artwork is used:
1. Record the source.
2. Record license.
3. Save attribution requirements.
4. Prefer local copies rather than fragile hotlinks.

Every interactive feature should have a meaningful purpose related to BioEar.
